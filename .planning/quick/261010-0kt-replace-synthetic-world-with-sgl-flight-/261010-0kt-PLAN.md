## Locked data facts (use verbatim; do not re-derive)

- Dataset: DAF-MIT AIA / Sander Geophysics Ltd. (SGL) 2020 flight data, Zenodo DOI 10.5281/zenodo.12723700, 10 Hz HDF5, Cessna Grand Caravan near Ottawa. Ships LICENSE.md (public domain, 17 U.S.C. 105) and DATA_SHARING_AGREEMENT.md (non-profit research/education only).
- Clean reference (truth): Mag 1 tail stinger, `mag_1_c` (compensated) and `mag_1_igrf` (IGRF + diurnal corrected).
- Polluted sensors: cabin `mag_3_uc` (mid-cabin near INS), `mag_4_uc` (rear floor), `mag_5_uc` (rear ceiling).
- Nuisance channels: fluxgates `flux_a..d` (x, y, z, t); current sensors `cur_*` (radio, fans, fuel pump, flap motor, servos, batteries).
- Position truth: `lat`/`lon`, `utm_x`/`utm_y` (WGS-84 UTM 18N), `utm_z`. Attitude: `ins_pitch`/`ins_roll`/`ins_yaw`. INS velocities: `ins_vn`, `ins_vw`.
- Calibration line: Flt1006 line 1006.08 (Eastern Ontario Tolles-Lawson calibration at 400 m, tt 55770–56609).
- Held-out lines: Flt1003 line 1003.02 (Eastern Ontario free-fly at 400 m, tt 50713–54497, ~63 min) and Flt1007 line 1007.06 (Renfrew free-fly at 400 m, tt 57770–63010).
- INS drift must be SIMULATED by integrating `ins_vn`/`ins_vw` with added bias + random walk. The recorded INS channels are GPS-aided and do not drift.
- Maps: MagNav.jl `ottawa_area_maps` artifact (Dropbox link in MagNav.jl Artifacts.toml): `Eastern_395.h5`, `Renfrew_395.h5`, 395 m HAE, nT grids with lon/lat vectors and a validity mask. k-NN-filled regions are not real data and must never be navigated through. EMAG2v3 (NOAA, DOI 10.7289/V5H70CVX, 2 arc-min, 4 km altitude) is optional coarse-map comparison only, never the navigation map.
- Raw files are 63–123 MB each, live in `data_raw/` (gitignored, never shipped to Qollab). Current state of `data_raw/`: Flt1006_train.h5 (partial or complete), LICENSE.md, DATA_SHARING_AGREEMENT.md, datasheet PDF. Flt1003, Flt1007 and both maps are NOT yet downloaded, and `data_raw/ottawa_area_maps/` is empty.
